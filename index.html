⚖️ Weigh-in / line-set charge

Adders per Lennox install literature (5/16" 0.40 / 3/8" 0.60 / 1/2" 1.00 oz per ft); Goodman, Daikin and Trane publish the same 0.60 oz/ft for 3/8" liquid line. Nameplate charge and factory-covered length come off the unit's own data plate. Mini-splits state their own adder in g/m — use Custom.

⏱️ Clocking the gas meter (furnace input)

Shut off every OTHER gas appliance first (water heater will ruin the reading). 1,000 BTU/cu ft is the usual natural-gas default — the utility can give the exact local heating value. Firing more than ~5% over nameplate: check manifold pressure and orifices before anything else.

🛢️ Refrigerant cylinder pressure check

Valid only with LIQUID in the tank, settled to air temperature (not fresh out of a hot truck or sun). Reading well ABOVE the expected static pressure = air/non-condensables in the tank or it's hotter than you think. Well below = the tank is down to vapor. Recovery cylinders: never past 80% of rated capacity, BY WEIGHT, on a scale — never by pressure or by ear.
