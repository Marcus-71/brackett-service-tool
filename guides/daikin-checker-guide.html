Daikin Checker: from recording to diagram

Record a Daikin inverter system with the Bluetooth service checker, open the recording in the Service Tool, and see the whole run on a refrigerant-circuit diagram with a list of what to check. Works on your phone, offline, and nothing is uploaded.

Daikin service checker app + BTSC Any phone with email About 2 minutes after the recording

On the job

  1. Record the system in Daikin's app the way you normally do with the Bluetooth checker.

    Let it run long enough to see what you're chasing: at least 10–15 minutes at steady operation, longer for a short-cycling or intermittent complaint.

  2. Save the recording and email it to yourself from Daikin's app.

    It attaches as a small .tgz file. A D-Checker .csv works too.

  3. Open that email on your phone and download the attachment.

    It lands in Downloads.

  4. Open the Service Tool › Daikin Checker › Open a recording, and pick the file.

    It opens straight on the diagram. On a laptop you can drag the file onto that box instead.

  5. Read What to check at the top, then use Show on diagram to see the moment it's talking about.

Red edge = likely to need a repair (charge, restriction, reversing valve, floodback, error codes). Amber = worth checking, including sensors that disagree. Grey = a data glitch in the recording.

Good to know

  • Confirm before you condemn. The card works from the unit's own sensors. A loose or failed thermistor can look like a refrigerant problem; the card flags sensor disagreements separately.
  • Check the refrigerant. Daikin's recording doesn't say which refrigerant the unit uses. The viewer works it out from the unit's own pressures and temperatures (R-410A or R-32). Make sure it matches the nameplate.
  • Error codes the customer never saw. Some codes clear themselves and never reach the thermostat. The recording keeps them, and that's often the clue for an intermittent callback.
  • Start-up and defrost are noisy. Brief swings in the first few minutes of a run or right after defrost are normal. The card ignores most of it; if an item only shows around defrost, weigh it lightly.
  • Other tabs. Trends graphs any reading over time; Runs summarizes each compressor run; Data has the raw numbers.
  • No recording yet? Tap Open Cycle Viewer (demo run) on the Daikin Checker tab to try it on a sample heating log.

What it doesn't do yet

  • No live readings from the checker. Record in Daikin's app first, then open the recording here.
  • One outdoor and one indoor unit. VRV systems use the Daikin Service Checker TYPE4 on a laptop; see its card on the Daikin Checker tab.
